Membership Terms & Conditions

Official policy, allocation guidelines, and participation standards.

  • 01

    Platform Network Access

    Talent Forge is a professional capability network operated by Alpha Auron Private Limited. Membership fees grant platform onboarding, verified tech credentialing, and eligibility for commercial project consideration .

  • 02

    No Fixed Salary or Guaranteed Employment

    Membership does not constitute an employment contract, permanent staff position, or a fixed monthly salary. Compensation is strictly project-based.

    Project Allocation & Work Orders

    Assignments are subject to active client work orders, tech competency match, assessment scores, and tier selection. Membership provides project consideration, not automatic placement.

  • 04

    Milestone Deliverable Compensation

    All project payouts are milestone-dependent and released directly following code reviews, quality assurance verification, and deliverable sign-off.

  • 05

    Profile & KYC Verification

    Members agree to complete identity verification, technical screening, and portfolio validation before work order allocation.
