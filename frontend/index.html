Frequently Asked Questions

Official guidance on Talent Forge membership, project allocation, and compensation.

1. What is Talent Forge?

Talent Forge is Alpha Auron's professional talent and project ecosystem designed for freelancers, students, graduates, and professionals to build a verified track record and access eligible commercial deliverables.

2. Who can join Talent Forge?

Any technical practitioner, developer, designer, or data specialist who wants to work on real deliverables and agrees to undergo skill and KYC verification.

3. What are the available membership plans?

There are five sequential levels: Level 1 (Starter), Level 2 (Professional), Level 3 (Specialist), Level 4 (Elite), and Level 5 (Auron). All levels are available in 6-month and 12-month membership cycles.

4. How much does membership cost?

6-month memberships range from ₹7,500 (Starter) to ₹56,500 (Auron). 12-month memberships range from ₹12,000 (Starter) to ₹1,10,000 (Auron) with project payouts released on verified deliverable completion.

5. How does project allocation work?

Deliverables arrive based on client demand. Projects are matched against your verified skills, tier level, and availability. Membership makes you eligible, not entitled.

6. Will every member receive a monthly project?

No. Talent Forge does not promise guaranteed monthly projects, fixed monthly income, or automatic payouts. Payouts depend strictly on active client demand and deliverable approvals.

7. How does project compensation work?

Compensation is project-based and established in individual project work agreements. Once completed work passes code review and QA testing, the agreed milestone payout is released.

8. When are payouts processed?

Payouts are processed directly after the agreed milestone deliverables satisfy documented scope conditions and client signoff.

9. How do I complete enrolment?

Submit the enquiry form on this page. Our Talent Forge Associate will review your technical discipline and send the official registration link to the Alpha Auron onboarding portal.

Yes. You can contact your assigned Associate directly via the contact buttons provided below.
